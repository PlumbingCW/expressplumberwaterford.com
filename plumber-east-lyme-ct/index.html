Many East Lyme homes rely on town water from wells, which has its own quirks. This page explains what those are and when a problem belongs to the utility or to you.

East Lyme water: what the utility reports

The East Lyme Water and Sewer Commission says customers depend on seven groundwater wells and about 114 miles of distribution piping. Six wells are filtered for iron and manganese, and the water is treated with chlorine, a pH adjuster and fluoride.

The report lists system hardness between about 21 and 50 ppm as calcium carbonate, which is soft. In practice, that means mineral scale builds slowly, so a water heater tank here is more likely to fail from age and corrosion than from heavy scale.

Manganese reached levels above the aesthetic limit in system results, which the report describes as a staining concern and not a health standard. If you see black or brown flecks in a tub or a toilet, a whole house filter or a cleaning routine may help, and we can discuss it on a visit.

Sodium was measured at about 43 ppm in 2023 and attributed to natural sources and road salt. People on sodium restricted diets should ask their physician, and the report itself advises that.

Water heaters and basements

A water heater that fails is a common emergency because the tank can release a full load of water onto the floor. Look for rust at the base, a wet floor or a rumbling noise during heating.

If your heater is more than a decade old, plan a replacement before it fails. Our pages on water heater repair and water heater replacement explain how we decide between the two.

For a basement that takes on water in heavy rain, check the sump pit and the pump. We repair and replace pumps and add battery backups, as described on our sump pump page.

Permits and the town

The town states that structural changes and interior remodels need a building permit, and that its Building Department is part of the Land Use offices at Town Hall in Niantic. If you plan a new bath or a basement project, ask there what applies.

For a leak, a clog or a failed heater swap, we explain whether paperwork is likely and keep it simple.

Code reference for East Lyme

Permit questions in East Lyme fall under the Connecticut State Building Code. The state's published code page lists the 2022 edition, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). We design repairs and installs to that standard and tell you when the town is likely to ask for a permit.
