Sewer, septic and well questions

Not every Montville property uses the same system. The town has a Water Pollution Control Authority with a treatment plant on Pink Row in Uncasville, which handles public sewer. Homes outside the sewer area use private systems, so your first question is which kind you have.

If you are on public sewer, a backup in a lower fixture can come from your own lateral or from the town line. A camera look separates the two, and we describe it on our sewer inspection page. If you are on septic, a backup may mean a full tank or a failing field, which is a different trade, and we tell you when that is the case.

For a house on a private well, loss of pressure may mean a failing pump or pressure tank. We handle the plumbing side and tell you plainly when a well contractor is the right call.

Businesses and large properties

Montville includes major commercial properties, with the Mohegan Sun resort in Uncasville among them. Smaller businesses nearby, from restaurants to offices, face the same plumbing demands on a smaller scale.

We handle commercial calls such as backed-up floor drains, grease problems and water heater failures. See commercial plumbing and hydro jetting for how we clear lines in a business without long shutdowns.

Preparing a Montville home for the cold months

A little planning prevents most winter calls.

  • Find and test the main shutoff before the first freeze.
  • Insulate pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls.
  • Disconnect outdoor hoses and shut off outside spigots.
  • Know whether your water comes from the town or a private well.
  • Keep a flashlight and our number near the panel in case of a night emergency.
  • Check the sump pit and pump if the basement has one.

Permits in Montville

Montville's Building Department is at Town Hall on the Norwich-New London Turnpike in Uncasville. The town says a permit is required to install, alter, repair or replace a plumbing system in any building, including one and two family homes.

The department takes applications online. If a contractor does the work, the town asks for a licensed contractor authorization form and workers compensation forms, and we handle those for the jobs we do.

Code reference for Montville

Montville enforces the Connecticut State Building Code, whose 2022 edition adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) per the state's published code page. Plumbing in a house on well and septic follows the same code for fixtures and drains, with health rules added for the septic side.
