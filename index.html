Before you call

Prices and emergencies, explained

What changes the price of an emergency plumbing call

Every emergency is different, so we explain the price before the work begins. These are the factors that move it up or down.

  • The cause and the parts needed, from a valve to a section of pipe.
  • Access, such as a crawlspace, a finished wall or a buried line.
  • Time of day, with nights and holidays handled by the same 24/7 line.
  • Whether the quick fix is enough or a larger repair is better value.
  • Permits for larger work, which the Waterford Building Department explains.

Pipe burst or sewage backing up?

Shut the main water valve. Keep people away from standing water. Call now.

When to call the emergency line

Call the 24/7 line when water cannot be stopped, sewage is backing up, a pipe has split or there is no heat or hot water in cold weather. For a slow drip or a planned upgrade, a scheduled visit costs less and works just as well. An emergency plumber can be dispatched the same day when available.

  • Active leak or burst pipe.
  • Sewage backing up through a floor drain or tub.
  • Water heater leaking or failed.
  • Sump pump down during a storm.
  • Gas smell: leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first.

The code behind the work

According to the Connecticut Office of the State Building Inspector, the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) for permit applications filed from October 1, 2022. A newer edition may replace it, so we confirm with the Waterford Building Department before larger jobs.

Waterford, neighborhood by neighborhood

Where we work in Waterford

Boston Post Road, also known as Route 1, is the main commercial corridor through town. Restaurants, shops and offices along it need drains and water heaters that work on a schedule, so we handle commercial calls and plan noisy work around business hours. See commercial plumbing.

Shoreline areas such as Great Neck and Mago Point are close to the water, and the Niantic River forms the western border of town. Homes near the water are often closed for part of the year, which makes a winter shutdown plan and a known main valve worth the effort. A burst pipe in a closed house can run for days before anyone sees it, and that is where emergency burst pipe repair helps.

Quaker Hill, Jordan, Oswegatchie, Cohanzie Hill, Morningside Park and the Millstone area each have their own housing, so we ask your address and age of the home on the call. Parks and landmarks such as Harkness Memorial State Park, the Connecticut College Arboretum, the Eugene O'Neill Theater Center, the Waterford Speedbowl and Crystal Mall mark the places most residents know. We use them to describe where we are headed when you call.

Waterford is also next to Groton and Ledyard, which we serve on the outer edge of our area. If your town is not listed here, call and ask.
