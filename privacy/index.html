Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens when you visit this site or call our line. Updated 2026-11-16.

This site does not use forms and does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect personal details through the pages themselves.

When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We use the information to respond to your request and to improve our service, and we do not sell it.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the pages requested and the time. Third party maps on this site are embedded from Google Maps and may set their own cookies under their own policy.

For questions, write to contact@expressplumberwaterford.com.
