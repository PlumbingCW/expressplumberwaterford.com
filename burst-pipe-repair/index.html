A burst pipe is a race against water

Emergency burst pipe repair in Waterford, CT is about stopping the water first, then repairing the pipe, then protecting the house. Every minute matters, because a split supply line can release gallons per minute. Call our 24/7 line the moment you see it.

Winter freezes are the best-known cause, but pipes also burst from corrosion, high water pressure, a failed fitting or a nail through a line during a remodel. We repair the break and check why it happened so it does not repeat.

What to do in the first five minutes

These steps limit damage while a plumber is on the way.

Why pipes burst

Freeze breaks usually happen in unheated spaces such as exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages. Ice expands in the pipe and the split often appears a short distance from the ice blockage, not at the freeze point. The damage shows when the ice melts and water flows.

Corrosion and age weaken pipes from the inside. A pinhole may open first, followed by a wider failure. If a house has had several leaks, repiping may be the better answer than another patch.

High pressure is a hidden cause. A failed pressure reducing valve can overload fixtures and supply lines. We measure house pressure during repair, so the new pipe is not stressed by the same cause.

Frozen but not yet burst

A pipe that is frozen but has not split can often be thawed safely. Keep a faucet open, apply gentle heat with a hair dryer starting near the faucet, and never use an open flame. If you cannot find the frozen section or see bulging, call us.

Insulating pipes in crawlspaces and exterior walls helps, as does keeping cabinet doors open on cold nights. For lines that freeze repeatedly, rerouting or adding heat tape is worth a conversation.

What changes the price

Burst pipe repairs vary with access and the extent of damage.

  • Pipe material and length of the damaged section.
  • Wall or ceiling access and patching.
  • Whether other sections show weakness.
  • Emergency timing, after hours or holidays.
  • Water extraction or mitigation, which may be a separate trade.
  • Insurance note: a sudden burst is often covered for the damage it causes, and sometimes for access to the pipe. Call your insurer promptly and keep photos.

After the repair

Once the pipe is fixed and water is back on, check the area again after an hour and again the next day. Look at ceilings and walls below the repair for new stains.

Dry the area quickly with fans and open windows if the weather allows. Wet drywall and insulation can grow mold if left, so call a restoration contractor if more than a small area was soaked.

Documentation for your insurer

A clear record speeds up a claim.

  • Photos of the break and of every wet area.
  • Our invoice describing the cause and the repair.
  • A list of damaged items with approximate values.
  • The date and time you first noticed the leak.
  • Receipts for any emergency purchases.

Code and permit note

An emergency repair comes first, and the paperwork follows. Replacement pipe should meet the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the Connecticut State Building Code adopts, and a larger repair may need a permit afterward. We tell you what applies before we leave.

Copper, PEX and galvanized pipe repairs

The repair depends on the pipe. A split in copper is usually cut out and replaced with a new section and fittings. PEX can be repaired with approved couplings, and galvanized steel often means replacing a longer run because the pipe is corroded beyond the break.

If the same house keeps producing leaks in different spots, we say so plainly and explain when repiping is the better value.

Waterford conditions and who to call

A home near the shoreline, such as in Great Neck or Mago Point, may include seasonal or lightly used spaces where a pipe can freeze if heat is lowered. If you own a second home or a camp, a winterizing plan avoids a spring surprise.

If the main valve will not close or the break is on the street side of your meter, the Waterford Utility Commission is the first call. For the break inside your house, we handle the repair.
