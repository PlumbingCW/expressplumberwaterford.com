How we cover each town

Niantic is the shoreline village across the Niantic River from Waterford. East Lyme is the larger town to the west, with its own water and sewer commission. New London is the city to the east, whose utility also supplies Waterford water. Montville is the town to the north and includes Uncasville and Oakdale.

Drive times are estimates and change with traffic and weather. The dispatcher gives you a live arrival window when you call.

Every page on this site is written for the people who live and work in these towns, and none is a template with the town name swapped in. Each town page lists the ZIP codes we cover there, the local water or sewer office to call for street side problems and the permit office to ask about larger projects. Use them to decide who to call first when water is where it should not be.

If you are not sure which page applies, start with the town where the property sits. A house on the border may be in a different town than its mailing address suggests, and the dispatcher can confirm coverage in a minute when you call.
