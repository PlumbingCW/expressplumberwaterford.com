When the pit overflows

Emergency sump pump repair and installation in Waterford, CT is for the night the pump stops. Heavy rain raises groundwater, and a pump that fails then can put water on your basement floor within hours. Our 24/7 line is open when it matters.

Waterford has 44.6 square miles of area, more than 11 square miles of it water, so basements near the Niantic River and the shoreline can see high groundwater. A working pump and a backup are cheap compared with a flooded basement.

Pump failed during a storm

If water is rising in the pit and the pump is silent, act right away.

Why pumps fail

A stuck float switch is the most common cause. Debris or a tangled cord can keep it from rising. A failed motor is next, particularly in older pumps that run often.

A clogged intake screen, a failed check valve or a frozen or blocked discharge line also stop the flow. Water that does not leave the house may drain back into the pit and cycle without gaining ground.

A pump is only as good as its power. Storms often cut power, which is when a pump is most needed. A battery backup or a water-powered backup protects against that gap.

Test your pump twice a year by pouring water into the pit until the float lifts. If it does not start smoothly, call before the next storm.

What changes the price

Pump repair and installation depend on the system.

  • Pump type and horsepower.
  • Pit condition and whether a new pit is needed.
  • Discharge routing and check valve.
  • Battery backup size and battery type.
  • Electrical needs.
  • Insurance note: standard policies often exclude groundwater and sump failure unless you add a water backup endorsement, so check yours.

Seasonal checklist for homeowners

A few minutes before storm season saves a lot of trouble.

  • Pour a bucket of water into the pit and confirm the pump starts and shuts off cleanly.
  • Clear debris from the pit and check the intake screen.
  • Confirm the discharge pipe is open and drains away from the foundation.
  • Test the battery backup and replace the battery on the maker's schedule.
  • Keep the owner's manual and the pump model number where you can find them.

Replacement versus repair

A pump that is several years old, runs often and has already needed one repair is usually better replaced. A new unit with a fresh check valve costs less than one wet basement.

If the pit itself is cracked, undersized or filled with silt, a new pump will struggle. We look at the whole setup so the fix lasts through the next storm and not only the next call.

Code and permit note

Pump discharge rules matter. The 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), adopted by the Connecticut State Building Code, covers how groundwater and sewage must be kept apart, and groundwater should not enter the sanitary sewer. The Waterford Building Department can explain what a new pit or discharge line needs.

Wet basements in Waterford

Sump pumps are common in basements with high groundwater. If your pump runs constantly, the cause may be a water table that stays high or a gutter and grading problem that sends water toward the house. Sump pump work often goes hand in hand with exterior drainage fixes that a general contractor handles.

Do not connect a sump pump discharge to the sanitary sewer. Public sewer systems are not designed for groundwater flow, and the Waterford Utility Commission can tell you the town rules.

If you want to waterproof a basement, a pump is one part of the answer, and the plumber and a waterproofing contractor can work together.
