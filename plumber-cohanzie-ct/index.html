A residential area between the mall and Quaker Hill

Cohanzie is a named area of Waterford that a local community article places between the Crystal Mall area and Quaker Hill. The town's own 1998 plan labels it on its map of the town's community structure. We could not find an official boundary map, so we describe it by streets.

That article describes it as quiet and residential, with parks and a fire station. Call with your address and what is wrong, and we will help you plan the next step.

Streets and names to know

The 2011 community article mentions Vauxhall Street, Vauxhall Street Extension, which was formerly Cohanzie Road, and Parkway North. The 06375 ZIP code also covers Cohanzie, along with Quaker Hill, Bartlett, Best View, Harrisons and Thames View.

Because the boundary is not officially mapped, give us your street address when you call. We will place it ourselves instead of relying on the neighborhood name.

Choosing between repair and replacement

A water heater that has failed once is not always finished. The age of the tank, the type of fuel and the cost of the repair all matter.

Read about repair and replacement, and consider a tankless unit if you are planning a larger change.

Water lines and meters

Waterford's Utility Commission lists a service line material identification tool on its website and publishes annual water quality reports. Use them if you want to learn what is on the utility side of your meter.

Our service line repair page explains who handles which part of the line, from the street to your house.

Permits and who to ask

Replacing a water heater, moving a drain or adding a fixture usually needs a permit. The Waterford Building Department, 15 Rope Ferry Road, accepts applications in person or through the CITY SQUARED portal. Ask the department before work starts if you are unsure whether your job needs one.

Code reference

According to the Connecticut Office of the State Building Inspector, the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) for permit applications filed from October 1, 2022. A repair in this part of Waterford is judged by the same code as one anywhere else in the state.
